# PROPOSED — NOT APPLIED

This is a **proposal only**, produced by task 7.2 of
`.kiro/specs/neural-experiment-pipeline/tasks.md`. It has **not** been written
into `.kiro/specs/causal-witness-pwm/decisions.md`, and no code, threshold,
band, or condition has been changed. `causal/overlap.py` is untouched. Applying
this requires an explicit decision by the spec owner; until then Section 12's
third condition stands exactly as written and the causal witness continues to
fail.

Evidence artifact: `results/diagnostics/overlap_smd_raw_vs_weighted.json`,
regenerated by `tests/diagnostics/test_overlap_diagnosis.py`.

## The finding

`decisions.md` Section 12's third frozen support condition reads:

> every **adjusted** baseline covariate has absolute standardized mean
> difference (SMD) < 0.10

`src/causal/overlap.py::_smd` takes two value sequences and no weights, and
those sequences are built from raw `PatientRecord.timeline.static_covariates`
reads. It therefore measures **raw, unweighted** covariate balance. The wording
"adjusted" is not implemented.

This is not a cosmetic gap. `decisions.md` Section 9's observationalization
mechanism *deliberately* selects on `baseline_cd4`, `karnofsky_score`, `age`,
`symptomatic_status`, `prior_opportunistic_infection`, and `prior_art`. Raw
imbalance on exactly those covariates is the benchmark's designed confounding
signal — the thing the pipeline is built to induce and then adjust away. The
current condition therefore fails whenever the mechanism works.

## Evidence

Real ACTG175 v3.2 cohort, master seed 20240101, 5-fold cross-fitted logistic
propensity. Weights: stabilized IPW (`P(A=1)/e` for arm 1, `P(A=0)/(1-e)` for
arm 0), clipped with the frozen `"primary"` 1st/99th-percentile preset via the
existing `causal.weighting.clip_weights` — no new weighting scheme introduced.

Both seed paths are reported because task 7.2's task text and the committed
`scripts/run_causal_analysis.py` disagree on the cohort (see "The n
discrepancy" below). The verdict is the same either way.

Every raw offender the propensity model actually adjusts for collapses far
below 0.10 under weighting — the largest, `baseline_cd4` at -0.3744, lands at
-0.0187, a 20-fold reduction. The adjustment is working. The one residual
offender, `baseline_cd8` (-0.1363 raw, -0.1323 weighted, essentially unmoved),
is *not* in `causal.propensity.COVARIATE_FIELDS`, which is why weighting cannot
touch it, and it cannot be added: it is the negative-control outcome, and
`decisions.md` Section 11.3 / `data.leakage_audit.assert_no_self_adjustment`
forbid adjusting for it. Its residual imbalance is what the negative-control
outcome exists to surface, not a defect in the weighting.

### The n discrepancy (resolved)

Task 7.2's task text states dev-retained n=378 with arms {1: 205, 0: 173} and
ESS {1: 187.1, 0: 148.6}. The committed `scripts/run_causal_analysis.py` seed
derivation yields n=383, arms {1: 201, 0: 182}, ESS {1: 187.23, 0: 161.00}.

The cause is a **seed-derivation-path difference**, confirmed by direct
reproduction: passing `20240101` *un-derived* to both
`run_observationalization_pipeline` and `LogisticPropensityModel` reproduces
every task-text number exactly — n=378, arms 205/173, ESS 187.0905/148.5527,
`extreme_weight_count=1`, and all six raw SMDs to four decimals.
`scripts/run_causal_analysis.py` instead derives them
(`derive_seed(master_seed, "causal_analysis_pipeline")` at line 311,
`derive_seed(master_seed, "propensity")` at line 327). Neither number is
adopted here. The committed script is the canonical path, so the task text's
numbers should be treated as measured off a non-canonical seed path and the
task text corrected — but that is a spec-text fix, not a decisions.md
amendment, and it is not made here.

Note that this also affects task 7.3: the "exactly 1 patient of 378 outside the
primary band" it is scoped to investigate exists only on the un-derived path.
On the canonical path `extreme_weight_count = 0` and
`no_unresolved_positivity_warning` **passes**.

## Secondary observations (not part of the proposed amendment)

1. **Five of thirteen adjustment-set covariates are silently dropped from the
   balance check.** `weight`, `sex`, `race_ethnicity`,
   `intravenous_drug_use_history`, and
   `prior_antiretroviral_exposure_and_duration` produce no SMD entry because
   `_raw_numeric_covariate` returns `None` for non-numeric or absent values and
   `smd_by_covariate` omits such fields entirely. The omission is silent: an
   adjustment-set covariate that is entirely unparseable is indistinguishable
   from one that is perfectly balanced. Worth a separate decision on whether
   the gate should require an explicit entry (or an explicit exclusion) per
   adjustment-set covariate.
2. **Committed-artifact tension.** Task 7.2 asks for a *committed* diagnostic
   artifact at `results/diagnostics/overlap_smd_raw_vs_weighted.json`, but
   `.gitignore` excludes `results/*` with only `results/.gitkeep` negated. The
   artifact is written and regenerable on demand but is not committable without
   a `.gitignore` change, which is out of scope here and was not made.
